WELCOME TO WACK-A-MOJI!

Are you ready for the ultimate test of speed and reflexes? Wack-a-Moji takes the classic arcade whacking game and brings it into the 21st century with your favorite emojis!

Whack those pesky emojis as they pop up from their holes. Choose Classic for a timed challenge, or Endless to keep going until your lives run out. Build combos for bonus points as the levels get harder!

FEATURES:
- Classic timed runs and Endless lives mode
- Combo streaks with bonus scoring
- Pause and resume mid-game
- Increasing difficulty with multiple levels
- Local high score tracking
- Music, sound effects, and optional haptics
- Accessibility options for reduce motion and larger targets
- Simple and intuitive tap controls

Whether you're waiting for a bus or just looking for a quick fun break, Wack-a-Moji is the perfect companion. How many can you whack?
